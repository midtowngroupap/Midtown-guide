Getting Around

The train station is a five-minute walk, and the airport is 13 miles away.

```
Parking is free and included. Your parking pass is in the unit. Place it on your dashboard within 15 minutes of checking in, or your car could be booted.

  • MARTA at Arts Center Station

    5 min walk

    About 800 feet from the unit. Trains run from the airport terminal straight to Arts Center Station, no transfer.

    • $2.50 one way, $5 round trip.
    • No Breeze card needed. Tap a contactless credit or debit card, or a phone wallet, at the gate.
    • Prefer a Breeze card? It has a one-time $2 fee on top of your fare.
    • Each rider needs their own card. Tap out with the same card you tapped in with, so you pay once per ride and keep your transfers.
    Open in maps

  • From the airport

    13 miles

    The drive is about 15 minutes without traffic and closer to 40 at rush hour. MARTA skips the traffic and runs straight to Arts Center Station, or take an Uber or Lyft.
```
